SUSTAINABLE LIFE

Four horizons.
One living Kingdom.

Small choices can protect the places that define us. Explore each horizon and discover practical actions you can take today.

01 Marine Horizon 02 Desert Horizon 03 Mountain Horizon 04 Urban Horizon
01
PROTECT THE BLUE

The Marine Horizon

The Red Sea is home to some of the world's most resilient coral reefs. Choose reef-safe products, reduce single-use plastic, and never leave waste on the coast.

  • Carry a reusable bottle
  • Join a campus coast clean-up
  • Say no to single-use plastics
02
RESPECT THE WILD

The Desert Horizon

Our deserts are living ecosystems. Stay on marked paths, carry out every piece of waste, and use water with the care a dry landscape deserves.

  • Take shorter showers
  • Report leaking campus taps
  • Leave no trace outdoors
03
KEEP HEIGHTS PRISTINE

The Mountain Horizon

Saudi highlands shelter unique wildlife and native plants. Travel lightly, protect vegetation, and share the trail responsibly.

  • Protect native plants
  • Keep to marked trails
  • Share wildlife sightings responsibly
04
REIMAGINE OUR CITIES

The Urban Horizon

Walk, share rides, save energy, sort waste, and plant native trees. A more sustainable city begins with everyday student choices.

  • Switch off unused lights
  • Sort and recycle your waste
  • Plant a native tree
